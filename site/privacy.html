Privacy & enquiries.

How this website works, and what it does not do.

Preview notice. The contact address and operating arrangements have not yet been finalised. This notice must be reviewed and completed by Peter before accepting enquiries.

Who is this website for?

This is an independent educational website for Peter Wade, covering chemistry, geochemistry and scientific learning support. Contact: Contact details are pending in this preview.

What happens when I fill in the form?

The enquiry tool runs in your browser. It turns the details you enter into an email draft. It does not submit those details to a server, save them in a website database or add you to a mailing list.

When a contact address has been configured and the website has been approved for launch, an “Open email app” link opens a draft in your email application. Your email provider may then store that draft according to its own settings and policies. You must review it and send it yourself. The “Copy draft” button puts the text on your device’s clipboard only when you press it.

What information should I include?

A name, reply email address, learning stage and a short description of the topic are enough for an initial enquiry. Do not include confidential research, medical information, student records, financial details, passwords or third-party personal information.

Cookies, analytics and hosting

The website code does not set cookies or use advertising pixels, embedded booking tools or third-party analytics. Fonts, illustrations and scripts are served with the website rather than loaded from external design services.

The hosting provider may process technical information, such as IP addresses and connection logs, when serving web pages. For GitHub Pages hosting, see the GitHub General Privacy Statement. A different host will have its own policy. Links to publications and other external websites take you to services with their own privacy arrangements.

Enquiries and learning arrangements

An enquiry is not a booking. Suitability, scope, format, fees, availability and cancellation arrangements must be agreed separately before support begins. The website does not accept payments or make guarantees about grades, publication or research outcomes.

A parent or guardian should make the initial enquiry for anyone under 18. Teaching arrangements and relevant safeguarding checks must be confirmed before school-age support begins.

Academic integrity

The purpose of educational support is to help learners understand concepts and develop their own work. It does not include producing assessed work on a learner’s behalf, taking tests, fabricating data or breaching institutional rules. Learners should check what external help their institution permits.

After an email is sent

An email you choose to send is handled outside this website. Before opening enquiries, the operator must provide any further privacy information required for the actual service, including the identity and contact details of the data controller, purposes and lawful basis, retention periods, recipients, relevant transfers and how individuals may exercise their rights. This page describes the website’s technical behaviour; it is not a completed legal-compliance assessment.
